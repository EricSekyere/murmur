# The floating pill

The floating pill is a small always on top window that shows Murmur's
status and acts as a quick mic button. It stays visible over other apps
so you can dictate from anywhere.

## What the pill states mean

| State | What it means |
| --- | --- |
| Idle | Murmur is ready; nothing is being captured |
| Listening | Recording your voice |
| Thinking | Transcribing what you just said |
| Waiting (dimmed) | Session is still live during a pause; speaking resumes it |

If you pause speaking for a few seconds mid-session, the pill dims and
its label reads "waiting". That is not off: the session is still live and
dictation resumes automatically the moment you speak again. The session
only ends for real after the Session Timeout of total inactivity (or when
you stop it yourself).

## Move the pill

Drag the pill anywhere on screen with your mouse. It remembers where you
put it. If you use multiple monitors, you can park it wherever it stays
out of your way.

## Find the pill

If you lose the pill behind other windows or drag it off screen, open the
Dictate view and click Find pill. This flashes the pill and pulls it back
onto your primary monitor if it has drifted off every display.

## Hide the pill

Turn off Floating Widget in Settings to hide the pill entirely. You can
still dictate with your hotkey or double-tap key; you just lose the
on-screen status light and the quick mic button.

## Live preview caption

When Live Preview is on, your words appear on screen as you speak, before
the phrase is final. The caption can sit directly under the pill, or near
the bottom of the window you are dictating into. Choose which under
Settings as the Live caption position. Each caption clears once its
phrase is delivered.

> **Tip:** Live preview does a little extra work per session to show
> interim text. For the absolute lowest latency, turn Live Preview off in
> Settings and your words will appear only when each phrase is finalized.
